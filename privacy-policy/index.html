1. Who we are

Landmark Flooring ("Landmark Flooring," "we," "us," or "our") is a flooring retailer and installation company serving Carson City, Reno, Minden/Gardnerville, Lake Tahoe and the surrounding Northern Nevada communities. Our showroom is located at 2085 East William Street, Suite 10, Carson City, NV 89701.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through landmarkflooringusa.com (the "Site"), by phone, by text message, and in our showroom — and what we do with it. It applies to everyone who contacts us or uses the Site.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us directly

  • Contact details — your name, email address, mobile or landline phone number, and service address.
  • Project details — anything you tell us about your flooring job: room measurements, the products you're considering, timelines, budget range, photos you send us, and the contents of any message you submit through our forms.
  • Communication preferences — whether you have opted in to text messages, and whether you have opted in to promotional messages.
  • Financing inquiries — if you ask about financing, we will direct you to our third-party financing partner. You submit your credit application directly to that lender, not to us. We do not collect, receive or store your Social Security number, credit report or bank account details.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data — IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring page, the pages you view on our Site and how long you spend on them.
  • Approximate location — a general region (such as city or metro area) derived from your IP address. We do not collect precise GPS location from the Site.

In plain English We ask for the minimum we need to measure, quote and install your floor — and our website keeps basic visit statistics so we know which pages are useful.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect Social Security numbers, driver's license numbers, or financial account numbers through this Site.
  • We do not collect sensitive categories such as health information, biometric data, precise geolocation, racial or ethnic origin, religious beliefs, or union membership.
  • We do not knowingly collect any information from children (see Section 12).

3. How we use your information

We use the information described above to:

  • Respond to your questions, quote requests and messages;
  • Measure, estimate, schedule, install and warranty your flooring project;
  • Coordinate with you about appointments, deliveries, crews and job status;
  • Process payments and keep business records required by law;
  • Send you promotional offers, seasonal sales and new product news — only if you have separately opted in;
  • Understand how visitors find and use our Site so we can improve it;
  • Protect the Site against fraud, spam and abuse, and comply with our legal obligations.

We do not use your information to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you, and we do not profile you for such purposes.

4. Text messaging (SMS) & mobile data

This section explains our text messaging program in detail. It applies whenever you give Landmark Flooring your mobile number and agree to receive texts.

Consent is opt-in and always optional

We only send text messages to people who have asked to receive them. You may opt in by checking the SMS consent box on a form on our Site, by texting us first, or by giving your written or verbal consent to a Landmark Flooring team member in our showroom or on a job site.

Consent to receive text messages is never a condition of purchasing any product or service from us. You can work with Landmark Flooring without ever receiving a text. Promotional text consent is collected separately from customer-care text consent, and you may accept one without accepting the other.

What we send

  • Customer care messages: replies to your inquiry, estimate and quote confirmations, appointment and measurement reminders, installation scheduling, delivery and crew-arrival notices, and job status updates. Message frequency varies based on your project and your conversation with us.
  • Promotional messages (separate opt-in): sales, seasonal offers and new product announcements. We send no more than 4 promotional messages per month.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Landmark Flooring does not charge for text messages, but your mobile carrier's standard messaging and data charges will apply according to your plan. Contact your carrier for details about your plan's rates.

How to opt out or get help

You can cancel at any time. Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. You will receive a single confirmation message, after which we will send you no further texts from that program. You can rejoin at any time by opting in again.

For assistance, reply HELP to any message, call us at (775) 297-3236, or email sales@landmarkflooringusa.com.

Delivery

Message delivery depends on your mobile carrier's network. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Our SMS program is supported on major U.S. carriers, but delivery is not guaranteed in all areas or on all devices.

Your mobile information is never shared

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All of the categories described elsewhere in this Privacy Policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Your mobile number and your SMS consent are used solely to deliver the messages you signed up for. The only parties who ever handle this data are the licensed messaging providers and mobile carriers that technically transmit the message on our behalf — and they are contractually prohibited from using it for any other purpose.

In plain English Your cell number stays with us and the carrier that delivers the text. We never sell it, rent it, trade it, or hand it to an advertiser. Text STOP and we stop.

5. Cookies & website tracking

Our Site is a static website. Here is exactly what runs on landmarkflooringusa.com and what each service can see:

  • Google Analytics 4 — we load Google's gtag.js script (measurement ID G-KPV3BK2621) to collect aggregate site-usage statistics: which pages are visited, how visitors arrive, and roughly where they are located. It sets first-party _ga and _ga_* cookies to tell returning browsers apart. IP addresses are anonymised per Google's GA4 defaults, and we use the data only in aggregate to improve the Site. See the Google Privacy Policy; you can also install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Google Fonts — the Site's typeface is fetched from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com when a page loads. Google may log the IP address that requests the font files. No cookies are set by this request.
  • Google Maps — we embed a Google Map of our showroom on the Contact, Free Estimate and Carson City pages. Google may set cookies and receive your IP address when the map loads on those pages.
  • Vercel — the company that hosts the Site. Like any web host, Vercel keeps standard server request logs, which include your IP address, the page requested, and your browser type.
  • Resend — when you submit a form on the Site, Resend delivers that submission by email to Landmark Flooring staff. The contents of your submission pass through Resend solely for that delivery.
  • Supabase — the secure database where we store SMS opt-in consent records (your phone number, what you consented to, and when). This is how we keep proof of consent and honor your STOP request permanently.

What we do not run: the Site loads no advertising pixels (no Meta Pixel or similar), no retargeting tags and no cross-site tracking. Because the only cookies set are the analytics cookies described above, the Site does not display a cookie-consent banner. We do not sell or share your personal information.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Our Site does not currently respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals, as there is no common industry standard for them; we do honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals where legally required.

6. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we never have. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising in exchange for money. We share information only in these limited situations:

  • Service providers. Companies that run things for us — our website host, email provider, messaging provider, CRM, and payment processor. They may only use your information to perform that service for us.
  • Installation crews and suppliers. To install your floor we share the details needed to do the job — your name, service address, phone number and product selections — with the installers, freight carriers and flooring manufacturers assigned to your project.
  • Financing partners. Only if you choose to apply for financing, and only at your direction.
  • Legal and safety. When we are required to by law, subpoena or court order, or to protect the rights, property or safety of Landmark Flooring, our customers or the public.
  • Business transfers. If Landmark Flooring is ever sold, merged or reorganized, customer information may transfer as part of that transaction. We would notify you of any change in ownership that affects your information.

The one absolute exception: as stated in Section 4, text messaging opt-in data and consent are excluded from every category above and are never shared with any third party.

7. How long we keep it

  • Inquiries that don't become jobs: up to 3 years, so we can pick up where we left off if you come back.
  • Completed projects: at least 7 years, to support product warranties, labor warranties and required business and tax records.
  • SMS opt-in and opt-out records: retained for as long as we operate the messaging program and for a reasonable period afterward — we are required to keep proof of consent and to honor your STOP request permanently.
  • Website analytics: retained in aggregate form only.

8. How we protect it

Our Site is served over encrypted HTTPS with HSTS enabled. We limit access to customer information to the Landmark Flooring staff and service providers who need it to do their jobs, and we require our service providers to maintain appropriate safeguards.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not send Social Security numbers, full credit card numbers or bank account details through our website forms, by email, or by text message.

9. Your privacy rights

Wherever you live, you may ask us to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you and how we've used it;
  • Correct information that is wrong or out of date;
  • Delete your information, subject to records we must keep for warranty, tax or legal reasons;
  • Receive a copy of your information in a portable format;
  • Opt out of marketing emails (use the unsubscribe link) or marketing texts (reply STOP);
  • Opt out of any sale or sharing of your personal information — though as noted, we do not sell or share it.

To make a request, email sales@landmarkflooringusa.com or call (775) 297-3236. We will verify your identity — usually by confirming details of your project or the contact information we already have on file — and respond within 45 days. We will never discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights, and we will not deny you service, charge you a different price, or give you a lower quality of service.

10. Nevada residents

Under Nevada law (NRS 603A.300 – 603A.360), Nevada consumers have the right to submit a verified request directing a website operator not to sell certain covered information they have collected. Landmark Flooring does not sell your covered information and has no plans to do so. If you would like to submit a request anyway, email sales@landmarkflooringusa.com with the subject line "Nevada Opt-Out Request," and include your name and the email address or phone number associated with your inquiry. We will respond within 60 days.

11. California residents

To the extent the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA) applies to us, California residents have the rights described in Section 9, including the rights to know, delete, correct, and opt out of sale or sharing, and the right to limit the use of sensitive personal information.

In the preceding 12 months we have collected the following CCPA categories: identifiers (name, email, phone, IP address); customer records (service address, project details); commercial information (products purchased or quoted); and internet activity (pages viewed on our Site). We collect these for the business purposes listed in Section 3, from the sources listed in Section 2, and we disclose them only to the recipients listed in Section 6.

We have not sold or shared personal information in the preceding 12 months, and we do not sell or share the personal information of minors under 16. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes that require a right to limit. You may designate an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf; we will ask for proof of that authorization.

12. Children's privacy

Our Site and services are intended for adults making decisions about their home or business property. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us at sales@landmarkflooringusa.com and we will delete it promptly.

13. Third-party links

Our Site links to other websites — flooring manufacturers, our financing partner, and our social media profiles. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. Please read their privacy policies before giving them your information.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our business, our technology or the law changes. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If we make a material change to how we handle your information — particularly our text messaging program — we will post a prominent notice on the Site. Continuing to use the Site after an update means you accept the revised policy.

15. Contact us

Questions about this policy, or want to exercise a privacy right? We're a local business and a real person will answer. Our website terms are in our Terms & Conditions.
