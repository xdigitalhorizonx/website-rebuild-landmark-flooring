At Landmark Flooring we believe a budget shouldn't force you to settle for a floor you don't love. Promotional financing through Synchrony lets homeowners across Carson City and Northern Nevada spread the cost of a flooring project into predictable monthly payments instead of one large up-front bill — and if you pay the promotional balance in full within the promo period, you pay no interest on it at all.

How flooring financing works & how to apply

The process is designed to be quick and low-stress, and it fits naturally into the way we already work with customers:

  1. Start with a free estimate. Visit our Carson City showroom or have us measure your space at no charge, so you know the real scope and an accurate number before you finance anything.
  2. Choose your floor. Pick the material and style you want from our premium and budget-friendly lines — financing covers both.
  3. Apply for the card. Apply online for the Mohawk Flooring Synchrony HOME™ Credit Card using the Apply Now link, or ask us in the showroom and we'll point you to it. Applications are subject to credit approval.
  4. Install now, pay over time. Once you're approved, we schedule your installation and you pay for the project in monthly payments instead of all at once.

Both promotions above are deferred-interest offers: no interest is charged on the promotional balance if you pay it off in full within the promo period. If any balance remains at the end of that period, interest is charged from the original purchase date. Minimum monthly payments are required and may not pay off the promo balance on their own. All terms are set by Synchrony and subject to credit approval — the full disclosures are printed with each offer above.

What flooring financing can cover

Because Landmark both supplies and installs every floor we sell, your project comes to you as a single Landmark Flooring invoice — which matters here, because the promotional offers require the qualifying purchase amount to be on one receipt. A typical project invoice includes:

  • Materials across every category — carpet, luxury vinyl, laminate, hardwood and custom rugs.
  • Professional installation with warrantied labor from the skilled installers we use.
  • Prep work like old-floor removal, haul-away, subfloor leveling and trim — the parts of a quote that surprise people most.

Ask us to confirm exactly what your quote includes before you apply, so you know which promotional tier your project falls into — the 12-month offer requires a qualifying purchase of $5,000.00 or more.

Budget and premium options — both finance the same way

One of the best things about financing is that it lets you weigh your choices on what's right for your home rather than purely on the up-front price. A more durable, higher-end floor often costs only a little more per month once it's spread out, while still delivering years of extra wear. We carry quality options at every tier, so you can compare honestly:

  • Waterproof, pet- and kid-friendly value: our luxury vinyl flooring is the floor we most often recommend for busy Northern Nevada homes and rentals.
  • Timeless warmth and resale value: hardwood flooring — including engineered options well-suited to our dry high-desert climate — is a premium upgrade financing makes far more attainable.

Not sure which direction fits your budget and your rooms? That's exactly what our free estimate is for — we'll show you the numbers and the monthly picture side by side.

Flooring financing FAQ

Do you offer flooring financing?
Yes. Landmark Flooring partners with Synchrony to offer promotional financing on the Mohawk Flooring Synchrony HOME™ Credit Card. Two promotions are currently available: no interest if paid in full within 6 months, and no interest if paid in full within 12 months on purchases of $5,000.00 or more. All financing is subject to credit approval.
What can I include in financing?
Your Landmark Flooring purchase — materials, professional installation and prep work all come to you on a single invoice. The promotional offers require the qualifying purchase amount to be on one receipt, so ask us to confirm your quote before you apply.
Is there a 0% or no-interest option?
Both current offers are deferred-interest promotions — you pay no interest on the promotional balance if you pay it in full within the promo period (6 months, or 12 months on purchases of $5,000.00 or more). If a balance remains when the promo period ends, interest is charged from the original purchase date. See the full disclosures above.
How do I apply?
Apply online for the Mohawk Flooring Synchrony HOME™ Credit Card at synchrony.com, or start with a free estimate so you know the real project cost first. Call (775) 297-3236 or request your free estimate and we'll walk you through it.
